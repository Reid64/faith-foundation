### RESOLUTION ACCEPTING THE RESIGNATION OF THE PRESIDENT AND EXECUTIVE DIRECTOR AND DECLARING THE OFFICE VACANT

**WHEREAS,** Ron Landers has served FAITH Foundation as President and Executive
Director; and

**WHEREAS,** Ron Landers has tendered his resignation from that office and from
the Board of Directors, effective _(effective date to be completed)_; and

**WHEREAS,** the Board wishes to record its acceptance of that resignation, to
declare the office vacant, and to provide for the orderly discharge of the
duties of the office until a successor is elected;

**NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED:**

1. **Acceptance.** The resignation of Ron Landers as President and Executive
   Director of FAITH Foundation, and as a member of its Board of Directors, is
   accepted effective _(effective date)_.

2. **Vacancy declared.** The office of President and Executive Director is
   declared **vacant** as of that date. No successor is named by this
   resolution.

   elected, the duties of the office shall be discharged by
   _(TO BE COMPLETED BY THE BOARD — see the note below; this repository holds

   reflect the current officers and directors. The vacancy is not published, and

8. **Further acts.** The Secretary is directed to file this resolution with the

## NOTE TO THE BOARD — WHAT THIS DRAFT CANNOT STATE

Paragraph 3 is left blank deliberately. **FAITH Foundation's bylaws are not held
in this repository**, and neither are its articles of incorporation, its IRS
determination letter, or any previously executed resolution or set of inaugural
minutes. A search of the whole tree returns exactly one reference to the
bylaws — a sentence in the Document Retention Policy published on `/governance`
saying that bylaws are retained permanently.

Consequently this draft **cannot** state, and no one should infer from it:

- what the bylaws require on a President's resignation (notice, effective date,
  whether board acceptance is even required for the resignation to take effect);
- whether the President's duties default to the Board Chair, to another officer,
  or to a person the Board must appoint;
- whether a vacant presidency affects the quorum for board business;
- what signing authority is affected, and what must be re-designated with the
  Foundation's bank, the IRS, and the Texas Secretary of State;
- whether any filing obligation is triggered — in particular whether the change
  of officers must be reported on the next Form 990, Part VII, and whether Texas
  requires a change-of-officer filing.

**Before adopting this resolution, retrieve the executed bylaws and have counsel
confirm paragraphs 3 and 4 against them.** Answering those questions from memory
or from the website's policy summaries would be inventing the Foundation's own
governing terms.
